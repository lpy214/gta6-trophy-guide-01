    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough, and no trophy needs online play. Work the stages in order: the story first, then strangers, activities, collectibles, crime and money — and finish with 100% completion.

      Estimated time: roughly 200 hours to the platinum (PowerPyx, via GameMapa). Difficulty: 4/10.

      Missable trophies

      Good news: almost nothing can be missed. After the story, free roam stays open, so every trophy except one can be earned whenever you like.

        Every other trophy is safe to leave for after the story. The 12 story trophies pop automatically as you play, and the stranger strands, activities, collectibles and crime trophies all stay available in free roam.
