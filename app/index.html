    The road to Vice Got a Price!

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, no online trophies and no difficulty settings. PowerPyx rates the set 4 out of 10 — the grind is the 100% checklist, not the story.

    The 100% checklist, in one place

    The checklist unlocks after the story (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion). It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion — and nothing on it is missable.

    Ground rules

    • Cheats disable trophies and saving. The 61 cheats only unlock after the story — keep trophy sessions cheat-free, and save before experimenting.
    • No mission replay, no New Game+. Keep manual saves at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots, 1 autosave).
    • Free roam after the story. Everything needed for 100% stays available; quest givers who vanish mid-story come back.
    • Optional missions disappear. Black icons with pink letters fade after a while — they do not matter for trophies, since far more than 100 exist.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy is even possibly missable. Everything else — story trophies included — can be earned after the story in free roam.

      Sources & method

      The trophy names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them, as listed by the trophy trackers. The guides are this ledger's own words — they draw on the sources below and cite them beside each trophy, never copying their text.

      What is still unconfirmed

      • Bad Luck, I Guess — no confirmed method yet; PowerPyx believes it relates to the Payday Loans store from the Prologue. Its guide says so plainly instead of guessing.
      • Fair Game — which exact animal pops it is still being confirmed by players.
      • Positive Mental Attitude — whether the missable Chapter 1 meditation is required is unconfirmed; the guide tells you to do it anyway.
      • Rockstar has published no trophy list of its own. The list here rests on Exophase and PSNProfiles agreeing on every name, description and grade, cross-checked against PowerPyx, GTABase, Push Square and GTA Zone.
